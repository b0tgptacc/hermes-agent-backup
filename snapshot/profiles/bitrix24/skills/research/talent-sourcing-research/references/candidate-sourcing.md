# Candidate sourcing evidence guide

## Evidence-first workflow

1. Convert the vacancy into three buckets:
   - **mandatory:** role-critical experience, location/work format, minimum tenure;
   - **strong plus:** differentiators such as deployed AI solutions;
   - **verification-only:** salary expectations, availability, and hands-on depth.
2. Search multiple source classes in parallel: indexed resume boards, professional networks, career profiles, and relevant communities.
3. Retain only individual profiles with stable public URLs. Do not count search snippets, company pages, vacancies, agencies, or unverifiable identities as candidates.
4. Open each retained profile when possible. Record only claims supported by its text; label everything else `unconfirmed`.
5. Deduplicate by name, URL, employment history, employer, and location.
6. Rank core-role fit first. Optional experience must not compensate for missing mandatory requirements.

## Resume-board constraints

- Resume boards may expose titles and limited cards publicly while reserving full profiles for authenticated employer accounts.
- Never treat a title-only card as a verified match. Include it only as a clearly marked lead that the user must open.
- If exact sourcing depends on employer-only fields, ask the user to authenticate in their own browser or provide an export. Never request or type a password.
- If the user requests only links, still screen visible evidence first. Do not pad the list with adjacent specialists.
- Preserve source status exactly: `actively looking`, `open to offers`, or `not looking`.

## AI as a hiring advantage

A tool-name mention is weak evidence. Prefer:

- a deployed assistant, RAG/LLM workflow, AI automation, or AI-enabled product;
- a clear description of the candidate's ownership and contribution;
- production use and measurable adoption, cost, time, quality, or risk impact;
- security, data governance, monitoring, and fallback considerations.

Keep AI-first candidates in a separate section when they lack the vacancy's core operational profile. State that they are adjacent rather than substitutes.

## Shortlist fields

For every retained profile record:

1. public name/title;
2. individual URL and platform;
3. location/work-format evidence;
4. current or last role;
5. confirmed mandatory matches;
6. optional advantages;
7. missing/unconfirmed requirements;
8. public availability and compensation conflicts;
9. evidence confidence;
10. recommended contact priority.

## Verification gate

Before delivery:

- open each URL and confirm it resolves to the intended person;
- ensure every factual match appears in visible source text;
- mark inaccessible details instead of inferring them;
- do not reproduce contact details or birth data;
- exclude non-job-related personal traits from scoring;
- deduplicate the final list;
- verify the stated candidate count against the actual entries.
