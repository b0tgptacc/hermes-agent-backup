# Evidence and visual verification checklist

## Evidence gate

- Define the outcome before searching: speed, quality, throughput, errors, adoption, or ROI.
- Prefer primary experiments and official institutional pages.
- Record design, sample, exact effect, metric, and limitation for every headline number.
- Keep surveys separate from causal studies.
- Include a counterexample or boundary condition.
- Register URLs at retrieval time and attach verbatim evidence for key numbers.
- Verify the research brief before slide production.

## Narrative gate

- Each slide answers one question.
- The deck moves from thesis to evidence, application, limitation, governance, and decision.
- Recommendations are labeled as recommendations.
- Internal cases are labeled as internal verified observations, not external research.
- Slide count follows the argument rather than a fixed template.

## Visual gate

- Render a contact sheet and inspect all slides together.
- Inspect evidence, risk, case, and source slides at full resolution.
- Check clipping, overlap, card boundaries, contrast, decimals, source IDs, and URLs.
- Confirm the visual hierarchy remains clear at presentation distance.
- Re-render after every content or source change; do not assume a text edit preserved layout.

## Artifact gate

- Read back the final `.pptx`.
- Confirm 16:9 dimensions and exact slide count.
- Confirm each slide has the expected embedded media or native elements.
- Confirm external hyperlinks on the source slide.
- Confirm document metadata.
- Record size and checksum when reproducibility matters.
- State whether an Office/LibreOffice render was performed.
- State whether slide contents are natively editable or image-backed.
