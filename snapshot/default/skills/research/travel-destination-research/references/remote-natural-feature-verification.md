# Remote Natural Feature Verification

Use this evidence pattern for an obscure bay, peak, trail, river, island, or similar place with sparse web coverage.

## Minimum evidence packet

1. **Identity:** exact name, region, feature type, coordinates, stable map-object URL.
2. **Cross-check:** topographic map, second gazetteer, or official area map showing neighboring named features.
3. **Relative position:** calculate straight-line distance to the nearest known city and 1–2 neighboring landmarks; label it `по прямой`.
4. **Access evidence:** mapped roads/trails, official route catalog, permit pages, operator listings, and recent trip reports.
5. **Infrastructure evidence:** settlements, camps, shelters, piers, communications, and evacuation options—if missing, report only that none were found in the checked sources.
6. **Operational status:** official open/closed notice, protected-area status, seasonal window, and date checked.
7. **Suitability verdict:** mainstream day trip, specialist detour, or expedition-only.

## Safe wording under sparse evidence

- “На проверенных картах дорога не обозначена.”
- “В официальном каталоге отдельный маршрут не найден.”
- “Автомобильную доступность подтвердить не удалось.”
- “Это указывает на удалённость, но не доказывает отсутствие местных троп или подъезда.”

Avoid categorical claims such as “дороги нет,” “доступ только морем,” or “инфраструктура отсутствует” unless an authoritative current source says so.

## Map inspection rules

A historic topographic sheet can verify coastline, relief, rivers, named bays, and then-existing roads. It cannot prove current road or facility status. Record the map edition or visible publication date when available.

OSM/Nominatim is useful for identity and coordinates. Missing OSM POIs are negative search results, not proof of real-world absence.

## Access recommendation

For remote coastal destinations, ask operators precise questions rather than accepting a generic sales claim:

- Have you landed in this exact bay recently?
- What sea state prevents landing?
- Is landing by beach, rocks, tender, or wading?
- Is there a verified trail or only cross-country travel?
- What satellite communication and evacuation plan is carried?
- Are permits, border-zone approvals, or protected-area permissions required?

For volatile conditions, recommend reconfirming access 24–48 hours before departure.
