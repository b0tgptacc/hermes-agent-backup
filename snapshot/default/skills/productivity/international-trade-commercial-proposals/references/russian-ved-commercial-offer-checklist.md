# Russian VED Commercial Offer Checklist

Use this note when the buyer, importer, payment, or customs leg is in the Russian Federation. It is a drafting checklist, not legal advice.

## Pricing and FX

- Prefer one clear currency of obligation (for China-origin goods, often CNY).
- Label RUB equivalents as informational unless RUB is contractually the payment currency.
- Primary rate source: Bank of Russia daily rates, recording the effective date and nominal.
- Transparent RUB settlement formula:

  `RUB payable = CNY obligation × official CNY/RUB rate on invoice date × bank/conversion coefficient`

- State when an unpaid invoice expires and can be reissued.
- Allocate payer-bank and correspondent-bank fees.
- Recompute the example with Decimal arithmetic.

## DAP Russia Allocation

For `DAP [exact address/city], Russian Federation, Incoterms® 2020`, explicitly say whether the quoted price includes:

- goods and export packaging;
- export customs formalities in the origin country;
- international and domestic carriage to the named place;
- cargo insurance, if purchased;
- unloading.

Explicitly allocate to the buyer unless the contract says otherwise:

- Russian import customs declaration;
- customs duty and import VAT;
- customs representative/broker charges;
- EAEU conformity assessment, declarations/certificates, and mandatory marking.

Do not confuse DAP with DDP.

## Production Timing Pattern

Use a three-condition start gate:

> Manufacturing lead time starts only after (1) advance funds are credited, (2) the specification is signed, and (3) all mandatory technical data are received.

Separate the timeline into:

1. manufacturing and factory quality control;
2. carrier booking and export handoff;
3. estimated transit and border/customs variability.

Add:

- capacity slot reservation period;
- schedule effect of late approvals or change orders;
- whether early completion and partial shipment are allowed;
- a total planning range clearly labeled as estimated.

## Pre-Send Fields

- legal names, registration data, addresses, contacts, and banks;
- manufacturer, brand, SKU, technical parameters, and approved substitutes;
- country of origin and HS/TN VED EAEU code;
- end user/end use and export-control screening;
- EAEU conformity and labeling route;
- exact Incoterm named place and unloading point;
- transport route and prohibited/transit jurisdictions;
- governing law, dispute forum, contract language, and document precedence;
- warranty start/end and acceptance evidence;
- quote validity and production-slot expiration.

## Artifact Verification

- Verify every line total, subtotal, freight line, grand total, and FX example.
- Confirm the source date appears next to the rate.
- Render every page and inspect tables, cards, footers, source URLs, and signature lines.
- Verify the final PDF page count independently; an extra page often indicates overflow even when individual HTML page previews look correct.
